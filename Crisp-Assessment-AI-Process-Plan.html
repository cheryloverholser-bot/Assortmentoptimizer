Crisp Assessment Process — AI-Enhanced

A living reference for running AI-assisted category management assessments. Each phase documents what the team does, where Claude helps, and what must be complete before moving forward.

Phase 1
Intake
Output: Baseline insight on people, process & tech
Phase 2
Discovery
Output: Workshop notes & current-state view
Phase 3
Analysis
Output: Prioritized opportunity log
Phase 4
Roadmap & Readout
Output: Solution roadmap & exec readout

How to use this site

Navigate by phaseUse the sidebar to move through the 4 phases in order. Don't skip a phase until its gate is cleared.
Use the AI stepsEach phase has AI steps — current (NOW) and new (NEW). Full prompts are in the AI Prompt Library.
Check the gatesEach phase ends with a gate. All criteria must be met before the next phase begins.
